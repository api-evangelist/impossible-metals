# Impossible Metals

> Impossible Metals is a deep-ocean robotics and critical-minerals company (founded 2020, San Jose CA,
> robotics lab in Canada, Y Combinator W22, certified B Corp). It builds the Eureka autonomous underwater
> vehicle, which hovers above the seabed and uses computer vision and robotic arms to pick polymetallic
> nodules one at a time, leaving visible fauna and the sediment layer in place. Eureka III is slated for a
> 2026 test in the BGR contract area of the Clarion-Clipperton Zone.

## What is and is not here

Impossible Metals is a mining and robotics company, not a software vendor. It has NO developer program, NO
product API, NO SDKs, NO CLI, NO developer portal, NO API pricing, NO status page and NO api./developer./docs.
subdomain — each of those was probed on 2026-08-23 and each is genuinely absent.

What it DOES serve, by default and without advertising it, is the WordPress REST content API behind
impossiblemetals.com, plus two OpenAPI documents its events plugin publishes on that same host. All of it is
anonymously readable, read-only, and free. That is the surface described below.

## How to call it

- Pagination: ?page= and ?per_page= (max 100). Read X-WP-Total and X-WP-TotalPages, plus an RFC 8288 Link header.
  The /tribe/events/v1/ surface is different — it paginates in the JSON body (total, total_pages, next_rest_url).
- Trim payloads with ?_fields=id,title,link and resolve linked records with ?_embed.
- Filter with ?search=, ?categories=, ?tags=, ?slug=, ?after=/?before=, ?orderby=/?order=.
- Errors are the WordPress envelope {"code","message","data":{"status"}} served as application/json.
  It is NOT RFC 9457 problem+json. Key on `code`, never on `message`.
- No rate limits are published and NO rate-limit headers are returned — not X-RateLimit-*, not RateLimit-*,
  not Retry-After. You cannot see your headroom. Honour cache-control and back off on any 403 or 5xx.

## Where the company's real data lives (not in an API)

Impossible Metals publishes environmental and test data from the Eureka programme as files on Google Drive,
linked from https://impossiblemetals.com/sustainability/public-data/ — the Eureka II April 2024 deep-ocean
test data and the Eureka III DHI collector-plume modelling report. There is no data catalog endpoint, no DCAT
or CKAN surface, and no machine-readable access to any of it.
